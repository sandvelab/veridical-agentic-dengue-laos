01_observation

What observation model do the counts get? Monthly province counts on this dataset are heavily over-dispersed and about a third of them are zero, and whether that is one distribution stretched wide or a mixture of two processes is a modelling choice rather than something the data settles.

Children alternatives

Only the main path is run by this node. The others are run by the stability node.

  • a_negBinomial not taken
    One negative-binomial distribution for every cell, with a single dispersion shared across provinces: the over-dispersion and the zeros are both absorbed by the same variance function rather than by a separate zero process.
  • b_zeroInflated not taken
    A mixture: a share of the zero months come from a process that reports nothing at all, and the rest of the record — zeros included — comes from the same negative binomial as before. The share is estimated rather than assumed, and if it comes out near zero the fork has answered itself.
  • c_hurdle main path
    Two processes rather than one distribution: whether a province-month reports any cases at all is a logistic model, and how many it reports given that it reports any is a count model fitted only on the months that did. Reporting and magnitude are then allowed to depend on the season and the climate differently.
